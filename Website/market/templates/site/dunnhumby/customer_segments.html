RFM and Churn Guide
RFM Segments Guide
Champions High value, high frequency, recent customers — your best customers.
Loyal Customers High frequency and monetary value — reliable revenue source.
Potential Loyalists Recent customers with good frequency and spending. Nurture them.
New Customers Recent but low frequency/monetary — focus on onboarding.
Big Spenders High monetary value, regardless of recency or frequency.
Regular Customers Consistent shoppers but not exceptional. Upsell opportunities.
Need Attention Good customers (high F & M) who haven't purchased recently.
At Risk Average customers who haven't shopped recently.
Can't Lose Them Your best customers who are on the verge of churning.
Hibernating Low activity customers who might be lost.
Lost Lowest scores across all dimensions.
Churn Risk Levels
Very High Risk Customers are on the verge of leaving — urgent re-engagement is critical.
High Risk Customers are disengaging — require immediate attention to prevent churn.
Medium Risk Customers show moderate activity — may churn if not nurtured.
Low Risk Customers are loyal and engaged — very unlikely to churn.
